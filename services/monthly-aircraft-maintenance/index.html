Recurring appearance care

Monthly Aircraft Appearance Maintenance

The current monthly plan provides recurring exterior appearance care built around a maintenance wash, bug removal, windows, and wipe-down. It is intended to coordinate routine cleaning rather than wait for heavier buildup before every appointment.

Monthly service remains subject to aircraft condition, location, travel, access, weather, and scheduling. It is not presented as a contract, membership, automatic-billing program, or guarantee of a fixed appointment date.

Currently Priced Optional Add-Ons

The homepage pricing selector shows three monthly-plan add-ons. Their amounts vary by aircraft category and remain unchanged:

  • Belly degreasing
  • Spray wax or sealant
  • Interior cleaning

An add-on is not assumed to be included unless it is requested and quoted. Condition, surface suitability, access, and available appointment time can affect the final scope.

Why Recurring Care Can Be Easier to Plan

Regular appearance care can make it easier to discuss a familiar aircraft, work location, access process, and typical cleaning scope than waiting until contamination has become heavy. It can also help owners coordinate bug removal, windows, and routine wipe-down on a repeating basis.

This is a planning benefit, not a promise that every visit will require identical work or take place on a guaranteed date.

Condition Still Matters

A recurring schedule does not make the aircraft’s condition irrelevant. Weather exposure, use, storage, missed appointments, access changes, and new contamination may affect what fits within the next visit.

If condition has changed substantially, the requested work may need to be reviewed as a different or expanded scope.

Scheduling and Access Coordination

  1. Confirm timing. At least 24 hours of advance notice is preferred for each appointment.
  2. Confirm aircraft condition. Describe material changes since the previous visit, including heavier bugs, belly residue, or cabin needs.
  3. Arrange access. The customer coordinates aircraft, keys, ramp, gate, hangar, security, FBO, airport, or facility permission as required.
  4. Review travel and weather. Availability may vary based on travel, weather, airport access, and existing commitments.

No automatic commitment: The page does not establish a contract, cancellation rule, automatic renewal, automatic billing, guaranteed discount, or fixed appointment date.

Choosing the Maintenance-Wash Method

The aircraft’s current condition helps determine whether waterless aircraft washing or another cleaning approach is appropriate. Light routine contamination may fit careful product-and-microfiber work, while broader buildup may require wet aircraft washing where facility permission and runoff considerations allow it.

Ozark Aircraft Wash brings its own equipment, RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft cleaning and appearance care, and its own water supply. Owners should disclose special coatings, finishes, transparencies, sensitive surfaces, and manufacturer restrictions.

Monthly-Maintenance Questions

Does monthly service require a long-term contract?

No contract or membership commitment is advertised. Scheduling and scope are coordinated according to current condition, access, travel, weather, and availability.

Are belly and interior cleaning included every month?

They are shown as optional add-ons in current pricing and must be requested and scoped.

What happens if an appointment is missed?

No cancellation term is invented. If more time passes or condition changes, the next requested scope may need review before it is quoted or scheduled.
