Category guidance

Aircraft Detailing by Category

Ozark Aircraft Wash uses five aircraft categories to organize general pricing guidance: single-engine aircraft, twin-piston aircraft, turboprops, very light jets, and light jets. These pages explain how exterior area, configuration, visible condition, access, and requested work can affect an appearance-care appointment.

A category is a starting point, not a guaranteed classification or price. Aircraft type, size, configuration, surface materials, special finishes, condition, facility requirements, and the services requested must be confirmed before quoting.

How Category Guidance Works

The examples in the homepage selector help owners recognize broad pricing groups. They do not represent manufacturer endorsement, factory authorization, model certification, specialized expertise, or a promise that every version of an example aircraft belongs in the same group.

Final classification can change when a particular configuration is larger or differently equipped than the general example. Share the aircraft type and configuration when requesting a quote so the correct category can be confirmed.

Suitability Still Requires Confirmation

A listed category does not mean every aircraft or work area is automatically suitable. Special coatings, finishes, surface restrictions, owner instructions, facility rules, aircraft position, contamination, and requested work may affect the recommendation.

Cleaning and appearance care do not include maintenance, inspection, mechanical diagnosis, or airworthiness determinations.

Products, Equipment, and Water

Ozark Aircraft Wash arrives with its own aircraft-cleaning equipment, RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft cleaning and appearance care, and water supply. Disclose special coatings, finishes, or manufacturer restrictions before service.

Travel and Scheduling

Mobile service is available throughout the region, with longer-distance appointments considered individually. Travel charges may apply based on distance and scheduling. At least 24 hours of advance notice is preferred.

Access and Facility Rules

Customers arrange aircraft and facility access and notify the FBO, airport, hangar operator, or facility when required. Work can proceed only where local permission, work-area, and runoff rules allow.

Use the Airport Service Area to review verified airport identifiers and location-specific access, travel, and quote-planning guidance.
