Exterior service overview

Mobile Aircraft Washing

Aircraft exterior cleaning is not a single fixed process. Surface condition, contamination, aircraft category, work location, facility rules, and owner instructions all help determine whether a waterless wash, wet wash, or broader exterior detail is appropriate.

Ozark Aircraft Wash provides mobile aircraft cleaning from Ava throughout Southwest Missouri. Longer-distance appointments are considered individually, with travel charges potentially applying based on distance and scheduling.

Waterless and Wet-Wash Options

Waterless washing

A waterless aircraft wash uses selected cleaning products and careful microfiber handling rather than a conventional wet rinse. It may fit light dust, fingerprints, bugs, and routine appearance maintenance when the aircraft’s condition and finish are suitable.

It is not assumed to be correct for every aircraft or contamination level. Heavier buildup or material that should not be handled through repeated wiping may require another approach.

Wet washing

A wet aircraft wash uses low-pressure application, aircraft-cleaning products, hand washing, and deionized water. It may be preferable for broader exterior contamination when the facility permits water use and the proposed work area can accommodate the process.

Ozark Aircraft Wash does not advertise pressure washing or assume that wet washing is permitted at every ramp, hangar, airport, or private facility.

Exterior Cleaning Considerations

An accurate exterior quote starts with what is actually on the aircraft and where it is located. Light surface dust differs from accumulated exhaust residue, grease, insects, or material concentrated on lower surfaces. Windows, transparencies, special coatings, polished metal, seams, antennas, static ports, pitot tubes, and control-surface areas also call for deliberate handling.

  • Bug and residue removal may be included with a wash when the condition fits the selected service.
  • Aircraft belly cleaning can be added when oil, grease, exhaust residue, or dirt accumulation requires separate attention.
  • Windows and transparencies require appropriate products and handling rather than abrasive household methods.
  • Owners should disclose special finishes, coatings, sensitive surfaces, and manufacturer restrictions before work begins.

Cleaning is appearance care, not an aircraft inspection or maintenance service. No mechanical, corrosion-detection, airworthiness, or repair conclusion is offered as part of washing.

Aircraft Washing Questions

Which wash method should I request?

Describe the aircraft’s general condition and proposed work location. Waterless cleaning may fit lighter contamination, while a wet wash may fit broader buildup where water use and runoff are permitted. Final suitability depends on condition and surface restrictions.

Do I need to provide water or equipment?

No. Ozark Aircraft Wash brings its own aircraft-cleaning equipment, products, and water supply.

Can the work be completed at any airport?

No guarantee is made. Work may be available at airports and private aviation facilities where access and local rules permit, and the customer must arrange any required permission and access.
