## Differentiation review

The hub organizes choices rather than repeating individual procedures. The washing overview explains the complete exterior offer; waterless and wet pages compare method suitability; belly and bug pages focus on distinct contamination and access needs; interior limits itself to verified cabin work; spray protection concentrates on temporary protection and its boundaries; and monthly maintenance focuses on recurring coordination and verified add-ons. Shared access, travel, product, and quote facts are stated consistently but are supported by service-specific sections and related links.
