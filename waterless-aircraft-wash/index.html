Waterless Aircraft Wash Service

Ozark Aircraft Wash provides mobile waterless aircraft washing from Ava throughout Southwest Missouri. Waterless cleaning can be a practical choice for suitable aircraft conditions, hangared aircraft, light dust, bugs, and recurring appearance maintenance.

When Waterless Cleaning May Fit

  • Hangared aircraft with light surface contamination
  • Light dust, fingerprints, and routine appearance care
  • Bug removal after flights when aircraft condition is suitable
  • Recurring maintenance cleaning
  • Facilities where conventional washing is not appropriate but waterless work is allowed

The cleaning approach depends on the aircraft's condition and finish. A wet wash or other service may be more appropriate when contamination cannot be handled carefully with a waterless process.

Careful Aircraft-Cleaning Process

We use RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft cleaning and appearance care, along with careful microfiber handling and attention around windows, antennas, static ports, pitot tubes, seams, and control surfaces.

Please identify special coatings, finishes, existing surface concerns, or manufacturer restrictions before service. No product is represented as approved for every aircraft surface.

We arrive with our own aircraft-cleaning equipment, products, and water supply.

Service Availability

Mobile service is available throughout the region, with longer-distance appointments considered individually. Travel charges may apply based on distance and scheduling.

At least 24 hours of advance notice is preferred. When service is requested at an airport, hangar, FBO, or private facility, the customer must confirm that the work is allowed and arrange access when required.
