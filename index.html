Aircraft are not cars. Transparencies, antennas, probes, static wicks, composite surfaces, paint finishes, leading edges, and polished metals deserve aircraft-aware care.

As a pilot-owned business, we approach each appearance-care service with respect for the aircraft and use RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft cleaning and appearance care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ozark Aircraft Wash based?

Ozark Aircraft Wash is a mobile aircraft detailing and appearance-care business based in Ava, Missouri and serving the broader Southwest Missouri region.

Do you bring water and equipment?

Yes. We arrive with our own aircraft-cleaning equipment, products, and water supply.

Can service be performed at an airport or hangar?

Mobile aircraft washing may be available at airports and private aviation facilities where access and local rules permit. Customers arrange aircraft and facility access.

Do you travel beyond Southwest Missouri?

Longer-distance appointments are considered individually. Travel charges may apply based on distance and scheduling.
