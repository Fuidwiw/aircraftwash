Aircraft Spray Ceramic Protection

Ozark Aircraft Wash offers spray ceramic protection after suitable aircraft washing or exterior appearance care. The finish is designed to improve gloss, slickness, bug release, and short-term paint protection.

Call 417-989-0976Text for a Quote

What Spray Ceramic Protection Does

  • Improves gloss and appearance
  • Adds slickness to suitable painted surfaces
  • Helps with bug release during future washes
  • Provides temporary, short-term protection
  • Can complement recurring aircraft appearance maintenance

The current service description estimates approximately 3–6 months of protection depending on aircraft use, storage, exposure, and maintenance.

Temporary Spray Protection

Important distinction: Spray ceramic protection provides a temporary protective finish and is not the same as a professionally installed multi-year ceramic coating.

This service is not represented as paint correction, a cured aviation coating system, permanent protection, factory approval, or manufacturer certification.

Available After Wash Service

Spray ceramic protection can be added after a waterless wash, wet wash, or exterior detail when the aircraft's condition and finish are suitable. Ozark Aircraft Wash uses RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft cleaning and appearance care.

Please identify special coatings, finishes, or manufacturer restrictions before service. Application suitability is reviewed with the aircraft's condition and requested work in mind.
