Aircraft Washing at Floyd W. Jones Lebanon Regional Airport

Lebanon, Missouri · FAA LBO · ICAO KLBO

Ozark Aircraft Wash may provide mobile aircraft washing and appearance care here by appointment when airport, FBO, ramp, hangar, and facility rules permit. Customers arrange aircraft and facility access and confirm an appropriate work area. Travel charges may apply based on location and scheduling.

Independent mobile service: Ozark Aircraft Wash is based in Ava, Missouri. It is not represented as being based at, affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Floyd W. Jones Lebanon Regional Airport or any FBO.

Planning a Lebanon Appointment

The City of Lebanon currently uses Floyd W. Jones Lebanon Regional Airport, while FAA records shorten the registered name to Floyd W Jones Lebanon. The page uses the city-facing name and verified LBO/KLBO identifiers without publishing operational airport details.

Lebanon is a longer regional trip from Ava, so distance, existing commitments, and expected service time are considered together.

Travel context: Lebanon-area travel is a longer regional appointment and is quoted individually.

Appearance-Care Options to Discuss

Lebanon-area requests can combine exterior and cabin appearance care when access, cabin preparation, and the approved work area are coordinated. A longer combined scope should be described before a travel date is accepted.

Access and Work-Area Responsibility

The customer may need to notify the airport, FBO, hangar operator, or facility; arrange gate, ramp, hangar, aircraft, or security access; meet Ozark Aircraft Wash at the aircraft; and arrange aircraft positioning through appropriately authorized people.

Do not send gate codes, security credentials, identity documents, or other sensitive access information through the website. Confirm access directly with the responsible facility.

Products, Equipment, and Water

Equipment, a water supply, and RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft appearance care travel with the mobile service.

Owners should disclose special coatings, finishes, materials, owner instructions, or manufacturer restrictions. No product or method is represented as universally suitable for every aircraft surface.

Aircraft Categories Considered

The website's current category guidance covers single-engine aircraft, twin-piston aircraft, turboprops, very light jets, and light jets. Listing a category does not mean every aircraft at this airport can be serviced.

A final quote uses the specific aircraft and requested work, including condition, configuration, travel, access, and location factors. Explore all aircraft-category guidance or review current pricing.
