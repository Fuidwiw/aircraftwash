Mobile Aircraft Washing in the Springfield, Missouri Area

Ozark Aircraft Wash is based in Ava and provides mobile aircraft washing and appearance care throughout Southwest Missouri. Appointments in the Springfield area may be available based on travel, weather, facility access, and existing commitments.

Current services include waterless aircraft washing, deionized-water wet washing, bug removal, belly cleaning, interior cleaning, spray ceramic protection, and recurring appearance maintenance.

Aircraft Wash and Appearance Services

  • Waterless aircraft washing for suitable conditions
  • Low-pressure deionized-water wet washing
  • Bug removal from leading edges
  • Belly cleaning and light degreasing
  • Interior cleaning
  • Temporary spray ceramic protection
  • Monthly aircraft appearance maintenance

We arrive with our own aircraft-cleaning equipment, RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft cleaning and appearance care, and our own water supply.

Access and Appointment Planning

Mobile aircraft washing may be available at airports and private aviation facilities where access and local rules permit. Ozark Aircraft Wash is not represented as being based at or affiliated with an airport or FBO.

Customer responsibility: Customers are responsible for arranging aircraft and facility access. Please notify the FBO, airport, hangar operator, or facility in advance when required.

At least 24 hours of advance notice is preferred. Travel charges may apply based on distance and scheduling.
