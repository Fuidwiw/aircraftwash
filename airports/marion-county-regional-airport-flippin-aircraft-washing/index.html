Planning a Flippin Appointment

Arkansas aviation sources confirm Marion County Regional Airport at Flippin with FLP/KFLP. This is a cross-state service request from Ava, so the proposed work area and reliable owner-arranged access should be settled before travel is committed.

Travel, weather, access, and the amount of work are evaluated together for a Flippin-area appointment rather than promising service on every requested date.

Travel context: Cross-state regional travel is handled as an individually scheduled appointment.

Access and Work-Area Responsibility

The customer may need to notify the airport, FBO, hangar operator, or facility; arrange gate, ramp, hangar, aircraft, or security access; meet Ozark Aircraft Wash at the aircraft; and arrange aircraft positioning through appropriately authorized people.

Do not send gate codes, security credentials, identity documents, or other sensitive access information through the website. Confirm access directly with the responsible facility.

Products, Equipment, and Water

The planned mobile setup includes Ozark Aircraft Wash's own water, aircraft-cleaning equipment, and RealClean Aviation Products selected for appearance care.

Owners should disclose special coatings, finishes, materials, owner instructions, or manufacturer restrictions. No product or method is represented as universally suitable for every aircraft surface.

Aircraft Categories Considered

Owners can begin category planning with single-engine aircraft, twin-piston aircraft, turboprops, very light jets, and light jets. Listing a category does not mean every aircraft at this airport can be serviced.

Aircraft type is considered together with configuration, condition, location, travel distance, access requirements, and desired work before pricing is confirmed. Explore all aircraft-category guidance or review current pricing.

Prepare a Quote Request

  • Airport name: Marion County Regional Airport (FLP / KFLP).

Marion County Regional Airport Service Questions

Which airport identifiers should be provided?

Use FAA identifier FLP or ICAO identifier KFLP with the airport name and Flippin association.

Does cleaning residue diagnose a mechanical issue?

No. Ozark Aircraft Wash provides appearance cleaning, not inspection, diagnosis, maintenance, or airworthiness conclusions.

Does Ozark Aircraft Wash service Marion County Regional Airport?

Service may be available by appointment when travel, weather, scheduling, aircraft access, facility permission, and an appropriate work area align.

What should be ready before calling or texting for a quote?

Provide Marion County Regional Airport, the aircraft category and condition, requested service, ramp or hangar location, access status, preferred date, and relevant surface or facility restrictions.
