Aircraft Spray Ceramic Protection

Ozark Aircraft Wash currently offers spray ceramic protection after suitable aircraft washing or exterior appearance care. It is a temporary protective finish intended to improve gloss, slickness, bug-release maintenance, and short-term surface protection.

It is not paint correction, scratch removal, a permanent coating, or a professionally installed full ceramic coating. Full professional aircraft ceramic coating is planned for the future but is not currently offered.

What Spray Ceramic Protection Is

This is a spray-applied appearance-care product used after suitable cleaning. It adds a temporary finish to appropriate surfaces; it is not described as a cured professional coating system. Suitability depends on the aircraft’s condition, finish, existing products or coatings, and manufacturer restrictions disclosed by the owner.

Ozark Aircraft Wash uses RealClean Aviation Products selected for aircraft cleaning and appearance care. No FAA approval, manufacturer approval, factory certification, or universal surface suitability is claimed.

What the Temporary Finish May Improve

Gloss

The finish is intended to improve the visible gloss and appearance of suitable cleaned painted surfaces.

Slickness

Suitable surfaces may feel slicker after application, which is part of the current appearance-care positioning.

Bug-release maintenance

The temporary finish is intended to help with bug release during future cleaning. It does not eliminate the need for washing.

Short-term protection

The service provides temporary surface protection rather than permanent, multi-year, or professional full-coating durability.

Measured Durability Wording

The current verified website wording estimates approximately three to six months of protection depending on aircraft use, storage, exposure, and maintenance. This is a qualification, not a guaranteed service life.

An aircraft stored indoors and maintained regularly may experience different results from one exposed to weather, frequent use, or different cleaning conditions.

What This Service Is Not

  • Not permanent protection
  • Not guaranteed multi-year durability
  • Not paint correction or scratch removal
  • Not a cured professional coating system
  • Not manufacturer-certified, factory-approved, or represented as carrying aviation-authority approval

Why Cleaning Comes Before Application

Spray protection is offered after suitable aircraft washing or exterior appearance care. Applying a temporary finish over dirt, bugs, grease, or other buildup would not match the current service description. The aircraft’s condition helps determine the cleaning method needed before protection is considered.

  1. Assess the exterior. Review contamination, finish, existing coatings, and areas needing additional cleaning.
  2. Select the wash method. A waterless wash may fit lighter condition, while wet washing may fit broader buildup where permitted.
  3. Confirm surface suitability. Consider disclosed restrictions and avoid representing the product as correct for every surface.
  4. Apply the temporary finish. Complete the requested spray protection within the approved appearance-care scope.

Ongoing Washing and Maintenance

Spray ceramic protection does not remove the need for routine cleaning. Aircraft use, storage, exposure, bugs, environmental conditions, and future wash methods can affect how the finish performs and how long it remains noticeable.

Owners interested in recurring cleaning can also review monthly aircraft appearance maintenance. Monthly pricing separately includes a spray wax or sealant add-on; that wording should not be treated as interchangeable with every spray ceramic request without confirming the quoted service.

Mobile Application, Access, and Scheduling

Ozark Aircraft Wash is based in Ava and brings its own aircraft-cleaning equipment, products, and water supply. Regional service is available, with longer-distance appointments considered individually. Travel charges may apply based on distance and scheduling.

Customer responsibility: Arrange aircraft and facility access, notify the airport, FBO, hangar operator, or facility when required, and confirm that the proposed cleaning and protection work is allowed.

At least 24 hours of advance notice is preferred. Availability may vary based on travel, weather, airport access, and existing commitments.
